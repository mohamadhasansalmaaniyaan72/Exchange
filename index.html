⚡️ This application is an all-in-one solution that allows you to:

1
Manage your stock market and cryptocurrency accounting.
2
Store your TRX digital assets in the application offline. Your asset password is also stored offline and encrypted using end-to-end encryption, and you can transfer your assets to another address.

Store passwords for applications and websites, as well as two-factor authentication (2FA) login codes (RamzBiz and RamzBiz-V2).
4
Compress images.
5
Create QR Codes (RamzCode).
6
Scan and read QR Codes (RamzCode).
7
Register and manage products and other items that have an expiration date, along with many other features...
